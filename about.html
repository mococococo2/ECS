Our Story

Built by DFW, for DFW

EcoClear Solutions was founded on one belief: that every apartment community in Dallas–Fort Worth deserves a property services partner that actually shows up — not one that hides behind 1-800 numbers when things go wrong.

Our founders spent years watching national valet trash providers underdeliver on promises. Missed pickups. Unanswered emails. Properties losing residents to competitors because of something as small — and as solvable — as trash on the breezeway.

"We knew DFW deserved better. So we built something better. Locally owned, technology-driven, and accountable to every property we serve."

Today, EcoClear serves thousands of units across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex — from luxury high-rises in Uptown to garden-style communities in the suburbs. Every crew is local. Every leader is reachable. And every single pickup is documented in real time.

We're not trying to be the biggest. We're trying to be the best in DFW — and the reviews, renewal rates, and referrals suggest we're getting pretty close.

Our Mission

Cleaner communities.
Greener planet.

Sustainability is woven into how we operate — not bolted on for marketing. We actively promote waste sorting, recycling partnerships, and reduced-footprint routing to minimize environmental impact while maximizing service quality.
