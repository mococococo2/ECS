The Process

How our valet trash works

A seamless, eco-friendly system that delivers doorstep collection you can rely on — every night.

01
Resident Sets Out Trash
Between 6–8 PM, residents place bagged waste in their leak-proof EcoClear container right outside their door.
02
Team Collects & Verifies
Our uniformed crew arrives on schedule. Every pickup is GPS-stamped, photo-verified, and time-logged in real time.
03
Report Delivered to Manager
Property managers receive nightly service summaries with full compliance data, violations, and completion status.
