The Routine

Three steps. That's it.

We designed this to be the easiest amenity in your building. No apps to download, no schedules to remember — just a simple nightly habit.

1
🛍️

Bag & tie

Place your trash in tied bags — doubled up if leaky or heavy. Max 20 lbs per bag. Recyclables go in the same bin, clearly marked.

2
🚪

Set out by 8 PM

Place your EcoClear bin just outside your door between 6–8 PM, Sunday through Thursday. Keep walkways clear for neighbors.

3
✨

We handle the rest

Our team arrives quietly each evening, collects everything, and takes it to the dumpster. Your empty bin stays by your door until morning.

What to set out — and what to skip

Keep your neighbors happy and our crew safe by following these simple guidelines.

FAQ

Got questions?

If you don't see your answer here, reach out to your property office or contact us directly.

When does pickup happen? +
Pickup occurs between 6–8 PM, Sunday through Thursday, on most properties. Your specific schedule is included in your welcome kit and may vary slightly based on your community.
What if I forget to set my trash out? +
No problem — just wait for the next pickup night. If you have an overflow, you can always take it to the community dumpster directly. Please don't leave trash out after 8 PM.
What if my bag is missed? +
Contact your property office or email us at support@ecoclearsolutions.net. We investigate every missed pickup with GPS logs and photo records — and make it right the next night.
Can I recycle? +
Yes! Bag your recyclables separately and mark the bag. We follow local recycling guidelines and our crew will sort accordingly. Flattened cardboard can be placed next to the bin.
How do I get a replacement bin? +
Contact your property management office. We'll provide a replacement bin at no cost if yours is damaged or missing. Please keep bins inside your unit during the day.
What about holidays? +
We observe major federal holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, and Independence Day). Holiday schedules are announced via email and posted notices at least one week in advance.
Can I opt out? +
The valet trash service is typically a property-wide amenity included in your lease. To opt out, contact your property management office — they handle all service modifications.
I have bulk items to get rid of. What now? +
We offer separate junk removal service for bulk items like furniture and appliances. Contact us at (214)-909-1824 or ask your property office to schedule a pickup.
